About

I am an AI Research Scientist at Meta MSL Lab. My research focuses on agentic post-training, including multi-agents, vertical agentic applications, and AI for science. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Texas A&M University and my B.S. in Applied Physics from the University of Science and Technology of China.
